Compact STM32H743 autopilot in KiCad: shared regulated 5 V BEC input pads, USB-C, microSD and underside solder pads.
V2 targets 40 × 40 mm with 30.5 mm mounting; ERC passes, peripherals are placed, internals remain unplaced/unrouted, and DRC has not passed.
